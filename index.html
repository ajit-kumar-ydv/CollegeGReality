💡 Why I Started This

"I saw too many students fall for 100% placement marketing traps."

Like millions of high schoolers, I used to scroll through shiny college brochures, paid YouTube reviews, and inflated placement statistics. Every college claimed an incredible average package and 100% placement records.

The realization hit when I talked to actual seniors on campus: the real median packages were often half of what was advertised, hostel restrictions were absurd, and the best learning happened outside the classrooms. The glossy marketing was completely detached from reality.

I built College Ground Reality to create the platform I wish I had—a single place where students can get raw median numbers, unedited student reviews, and direct senior guidance before spending lakhs in admission fees.
